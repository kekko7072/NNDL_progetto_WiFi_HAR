\section{Concluding Remarks}
\label{sec:conclusions}

Under strict testing on unseen environments, the baseline achieved $48.2\%$
accuracy. Supervised contrastive learning, a slower training schedule,
overlapping windows and multi-antenna fusion all improved the performance. The
largest gains came from overlapping windows and antenna fusion, which together
raised accuracy to $\mathbf{72.7\%}$ without changing the baseline network.
Contrastive pre-training also helped when the encoder was kept frozen, but
fine-tuning reduced its benefit. Overall, using more of the available temporal
and antenna information was the simplest and most effective way to improve
cross-environment activity recognition.
